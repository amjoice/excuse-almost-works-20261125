## The entry

The single line a gamer texts the boss to get out of work during GTA 6
launch week. `main.py` (standard library only) writes the line — 12 words,
66 characters, inside the 20-word cap and the 1–140 character gate — to
`/output/entry.txt`.

> Gas safety check, I have to stay in till twelve, landlord's orders

Why this line: a tired manager has heard every excuse twice, so the one
that almost works is boring, specific and imposed by a third party, with a
time bound — and the laugh comes from its own logic, the landlord's orders
quietly outranking the boss's. No setup, no explanation, no signature, no
dead grandmother, no flat tyre, no sudden fever.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
